# Unit Economics

Current-source production cost, active-user volume, provider invoices, subsidy spend, and service revenue are unavailable. Therefore no cost per user, gross margin, runway, APY, liquidity, revenue, or sustainability claim is made.

## Calculation definitions

- Monthly infrastructure cost is the sum of paid compute, storage, egress, managed services, monitoring, backup, support, audit amortization, and provider charges, net of separately disclosed credits.
- Cost per monthly active user equals attributable monthly cost divided by unique active users with at least one qualifying committed or completed product action. A zero-user month reports unavailable, never zero cost.
- Cost per committed transaction equals chain and transaction-serving cost divided by successful committed transactions. Rejected, read, and provider calls are reported separately.
- Sponsored acquisition cost equals actual sponsor and faucet value consumed divided by distinct activated users. Locked but unused budgets are liabilities/capital allocation, not expense.
- Service gross margin candidate equals recognized, auditable service fees minus directly attributable provider and infrastructure cost. Token price change, unrealized gains, issuance, burn, Treasury movement, fake volume, and user PnL are not revenue.
- Runway equals unrestricted governed reserve divided by measured net monthly cash burn. The current Treasury snapshot lacks governed obligations and external custody, so runway is unavailable.

## Required segmentation

Report free, sponsored, and paid usage separately for RPC, Wallet/AA, Pay, Quant/Vault, AI, Trust, Resource, Explorer, Faucet, and future Trading. Attribute provider/model, region, request class, success/failure, data retention, and abuse-control cost. Do not average subsidized high-cost usage into an apparently sustainable paid cohort.

## Subsidy and fee controls

Every subsidy requires sponsor, policy, product, per-user/global cap, expiry, abuse control, remaining budget, and audit trail. Every fee requires user-visible basis, payer, recipient, gross amount, provider/venue cost, net protocol amount, transaction/receipt, and refund/dispute path. Performance fees apply only to realized net profit above the persistent high-water mark after all stated costs.

## Release decision

Scale requires measured activation/retention, reliable task completion, bounded abuse/support load, positive or intentionally funded unit economics, and an explicit subsidy runway. Kill or restrict a surface when provider cost, abuse, support, reliability, legal risk, or uncovered liability exceeds the approved limit. No release decision can be inferred until the missing invoices, usage data, governed budgets, and capacity measurements are collected.
