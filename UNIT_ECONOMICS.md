# Unit Economics

The platform is an internal mandatory control plane, not a revenue product. Costs must be allocated transparently to protected services without hidden user spread or transaction economics.

Track monthly cost by active protected service and by one million accepted requests:

- compute, memory, storage, egress, load balancers, WAF, and DDoS protection;
- logs, metrics, traces, audit retention, and status delivery;
- Secret Manager, HSM/MPC candidate, certificate issuance, and signing;
- artifact registry, vulnerability scanning, SBOM/provenance storage, and CI minutes;
- primary, immutable, offline, and cross-region backups plus restore drills;
- incident staffing, external audit, penetration test, and bug-bounty readiness;
- provider free tiers, committed spend, overage rates, and jurisdictional constraints.

No numerical cost or margin claim is accepted without a dated invoice, metering export, or provider price source and its coverage. The scale/kill decision compares the measured cost per active service, security incidents prevented or contained, release lead time, restore success, provider concentration, and support load. Subsidy budgets require an owner, ceiling, and end date.
