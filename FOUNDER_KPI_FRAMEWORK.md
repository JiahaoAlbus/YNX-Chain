# Founder KPI Framework

The authoritative machine definitions are in `security-platform/kpis.json`. Every current value is `null` and every metric is `unmeasured` until canonical telemetry, provider invoices, or approved support records are connected. No repository fixture can become production KPI evidence.

Activation and retention measure adoption of verified security workflows, not page views. Task completion requires an evidence-backed terminal result. Crash-free sessions exclude handled policy denials. Abuse rate uses confirmed outcomes rather than raw alerts. Provider cost and gross-margin candidates use invoices and metering exports; estimates must be labeled separately and cannot replace measured values.

Scale requires all of the following: SLO and error-budget compliance, successful production-shaped restore and rollback drills, no unresolved critical vulnerability, sustainable provider and support cost, and growing retained protected-product usage. A persistent critical safety failure, inability to restore, uncontrolled cost, or mandatory-gate bypass triggers kill, containment, or redesign regardless of vanity growth.

No dark patterns, fabricated social proof, false rankings, hidden fees, or misleading risk language may be used to improve these metrics.
