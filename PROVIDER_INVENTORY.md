# Provider Inventory

The machine-readable inventory is `security-platform/providers.json`. Providers are limited to their stated authority and can never replace canonical YNX identity, chain state, balances, permissions, transactions, settlement, receipts, or release truth.

Legal, terms, and jurisdiction fields remain `not-reviewed` unless direct approved evidence exists. That state blocks production approval where the provider is mandatory. Authentication descriptions name mechanisms only; credentials and values are prohibited from the inventory.

An unavailable provider produces an explicit outage or unproven state. Local cache, previous responses, or deterministic fixtures may support development and tests but may not be presented as current provider health or public deployment evidence.
