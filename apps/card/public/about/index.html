FROM EXPLORATION TO APPLICATION

A deliberate flow. No automatic card creation.

  1. Explore as a guest

    Browse Overview, Card, Top up, Activity, Controls and Help. Try clearly labelled local demonstrations; these do not represent an account, funds or on-chain transactions.

  2. Choose your wallet

    YNX Wallet and MetaMask are separate choices. The web app discovers the installed provider; if absent, it offers installation guidance without opening a blank custom-scheme page.

  3. Apply for a TEST card

    Review Testnet terms, choose a nickname, use case, spending limit and security controls. Account approval and a verified backend receipt are required for activation.

  4. Fund only after verification

    An ACTIVE Testnet card may request an exact YNXT funding intent. You approve the transaction in your wallet; chain confirmation and Card API acceptance must precede credit.

Current verification boundary

Public exploration is available. This page does not prove wallet approval, backend activation or YNXT funding. Unavailable private services remain Degraded without disconnecting your standard wallet.
