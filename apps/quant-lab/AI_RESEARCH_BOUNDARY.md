# AI research boundary

AI may explain datasets and results, draft strategy source/tests/reports, propose
parameters, summarize evidence, and generate a reviewable patch. Every invocation
requires declared context consent and records provider, model, version, cost or
unavailable-cost state, input/output hashes, timestamp, and failure.

AI may not sign, pay, trade, swap, withdraw, select a champion, advance lifecycle,
change risk/capital, approve a Wallet mandate, bypass evidence, publish, or invoke
an execution adapter. Suggestions require a preview and explicit human
Approve/Reject record. Rejection changes no state.

No AI provider is integrated in the current Quant runtime. Therefore no model,
cost, consent, or availability success is claimed.
