BEFORE YOU START

Understand the boundaries.

Is Quant a live trading service?

No. Research, Backtest, Paper and Testnet are distinct stages. A saved schedule runs research, not capital execution. No guaranteed returns or mainnet custody.

Do I need a wallet to browse?

No. Inside the app, standard Wallet connection, private account authorization and actions are separate. A private-service failure must not remove your standard connection. Never enter a private key or recovery phrase here.

Where can I find help and privacy information?

The workspace explains strategy, research and execution boundaries. Public risk explanations are available without authorizing an account. Consult the official ecosystem for privacy and support.
