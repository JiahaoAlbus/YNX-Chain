BEFORE YOU START

Understand the boundaries.

Does Finance hold my assets?

No. Finance is not a bank account or a mainnet custody service. Testnet assets and provider Sandbox records remain explicitly separate. Returns are never guaranteed.

Do I need a wallet to browse?

No. Inside the app, standard Wallet connection, private account authorization and actions are separate. A private-service failure must not remove your standard connection. Never enter a private key or recovery phrase here.

Where can I find help and privacy information?

The app’s Support section provides its configured help, privacy and dispute links. Opening support does not authorize a wallet.
