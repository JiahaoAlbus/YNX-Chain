SDK-owned local pairing/session storage remains per origin; Relay transport is encrypted, but at-rest storage encryption is not verified or claimed.

Connection approval and browser sign-in approval remain separate. After Pair approval the user must explicitly Continue; the unique central RPC goes over that same approved topic/account/chain and returns to the original browser challenge/cookie and product PKCE callback. There is no personal_sign substitution or second phone business session. Explicit cancellation fences late proposals/approval and calls original SDK cleanup; unconfirmed cleanup is not reported as remote revocation. Existing durable business sessions are not revoked by mere transport teardown.

Approved invalid sessions and late approvals are retired through the original SDK. Provider identity is explicitly `isMetaMask:false`. Multiple restored sessions require explicit selection rather than automatic choice.
