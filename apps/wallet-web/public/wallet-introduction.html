Features

Accounts and backups

Manage wallet accounts with encrypted backup and recovery. Keep passwords and backups safe.

Receive and send

Use a receiving address or QR code. Review the network, address and amount before confirming a transfer.

Pay and scanning

Payment requests and scanning belong in the wallet. Payment requires available services and explicit authorization.

History and recovery

Preserve unresolved transactions during recovery to avoid false success or duplicate submission.

Help and privacy

Opening this page does not connect a wallet, sign in, sign a request or request device permissions. Open the Web Wallet when you choose to manage your accounts. Review each product request separately.
