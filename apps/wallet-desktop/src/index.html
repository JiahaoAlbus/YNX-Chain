Backup and recovery

YNX uses an encrypted dual-key file plus a separate 43-character recovery credential. A 12/24-word EVM, BTC or TRON phrase cannot restore these PQ keys.

Devices and connections

Lock and account protection

Wallet locks on focus loss and according to its actual local timeout. Locking does not erase a signed or unknown transaction.
