Privacy boundary

Your wallet is a key, not your public profile.

  • Standard wallet connection stays available if private YNX services are degraded.
  • No recovery phrase or private key is requested or received.
  • Private Social access requires a separate, explicit Product Session approval.
  • Rejected or failed approval creates no local or canned session.
